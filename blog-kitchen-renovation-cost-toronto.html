How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Toronto?

A realistic, tier-by-tier breakdown of what GTA homeowners actually pay in 2026 — and the specific choices that move the number the most.

By the NEVACO BUILD Team · September 24, 2026

The honest answer is: it depends heavily on layout, materials, and whether plumbing or electrical is moving — but most Toronto-area homeowners can expect somewhere between $25,000 and $90,000+ for a full kitchen renovation, with the majority of mid-size projects landing in the $45,000–$65,000 range. Here's how that breaks down.

Cosmetic refresh: $25,000–$40,000

This tier keeps the existing layout — same footprint, same plumbing and electrical locations — and replaces cabinets, counters, backsplash, and appliances. It's the fastest and least disruptive option, typically completed in 3–5 weeks. Most of the cost here is cabinetry and countertop material; stock or semi-custom cabinets with a quartz counter make up the bulk of the budget.

Mid-range renovation: $45,000–$65,000

This is where most full kitchen renovations in the GTA actually land. It usually includes some layout changes (moving a sink or relocating an island), new flooring, updated electrical for under-cabinet lighting and additional outlets, semi-custom to custom cabinetry, and stone counters. Timeline typically runs 6–9 weeks once permits and material lead times are accounted for.

High-end / structural renovation: $70,000–$90,000+

This tier involves moving load-bearing walls to open up the kitchen to an adjacent room, relocating plumbing rough-ins, full custom cabinetry, integrated appliances, and premium natural stone. Structural work requires an engineer's drawing and a building permit, which adds both cost and lead time — budget 10–14 weeks for this scope.

What actually moves the price

Four things drive most of the variation between quotes for what looks like a similar-sized kitchen:

Moving plumbing or gas lines. Relocating a sink or stove even a few feet means rerouting rough-in plumbing or gas, which is one of the more expensive line items per foot of movement.

Cabinetry: stock vs. semi-custom vs. custom. This is usually the single largest cost category, and the spread between stock and fully custom cabinetry for the same footprint can easily be $15,000–$25,000.

Structural changes. Removing a wall between the kitchen and living or dining room is popular in older GTA homes, but if that wall is load-bearing, it requires an engineered beam, a permit, and inspection — adding real cost and time.

Counter material. Laminate, quartz, and natural stone (marble, soapstone) span a wide price range, and large islands or waterfall edges increase the square footage priced at the premium rate.

Getting an accurate number for your kitchen

Online cost calculators can only give a rough range because they can't see your existing plumbing layout, your home's structure, or your material preferences. The only way to get a number you can actually plan around is a site visit where someone can look at your specific space and walk through the real scope with you.
