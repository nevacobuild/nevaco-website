About Nevaco Build

Design and construction under one roof.

NEVACO BUILD is a general contracting and renovation company serving the Greater Toronto Area, built around a simple idea: one team should carry a project from first sketch to final walkthrough. We work with homeowners, families restoring older homes, and realtors who need work done to a standard that holds up under inspection — not just photographs.

Every project is managed directly by our team, with a single point of contact from concept through completion.
