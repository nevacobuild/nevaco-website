Our Story

Built to carry a project, not just a trade.

NEVACO BUILD started from a simple frustration shared by a lot of homeowners: hiring five different trades for one renovation, and hoping they all show up in the right order. We built NEVACO BUILD to be the single team that carries a project from the first site visit to the final walkthrough — design, permitting, trades, and scheduling, under one roof and one point of contact.

We work across the Greater Toronto Area with homeowners renovating in place, families restoring older homes, and realtors who need work finished to a standard that holds up under inspection — not just in photographs.
